Updated diagnosis:

- The repo can use GPU when commands run outside the Codex sandbox.
- The 4090s are healthy and can be driven to 100% utilization by PyTorch.
- The tiny demo training only shows low utilization because it is intentionally tiny: small images, shallow model, small activation memory, synthetic in-memory data, and short kernels.

- For real FastWAM/Wan training, GPU utilization should be much higher because the Wan/DiT model is orders of magnitude larger; the limiting factors then become batch size, dataloader/video decode, checkpoint/logging frequency, AMP/bf16, and multi-GPU launch configuration.

Interpretation:

- `action_only` is easiest and gets the lowest action loss because all capacity is focused on action prediction.
- `joint_video_action` learns action and a low-resolution video auxiliary target; its total loss is higher because video loss is included.
- `idm_proxy` is intentionally harder because action prediction passes through a future-latent bottleneck, which simulates an imagine-then-act design.
- These results are a toy benchmark for learning and debugging, not paper-scale Fast-WAM reproduction.
